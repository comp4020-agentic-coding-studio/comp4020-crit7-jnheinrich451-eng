with Machine Learning courses moved into Artificial Intelligence. The supplied
2027 ARTIF-SPEC page confirms these current groups:

- At most 12 units from COMP6262, COMP6320, COMP6242, COMP6670 and COMP6528.

This confirms that COMP6670, COMP8600 and COMP8650 appear under AI in this
snapshot. It does not independently establish the old ML rules, the effective
